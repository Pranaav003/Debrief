How it works

From meeting to debrief
in 30 seconds.

01

Paste or upload

Drop in a transcript from Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, or any platform. Or upload an audio file directly.

02

AI extracts everything

Debrief detects your meeting type and pulls out decisions, action items, open questions, and a follow-up email draft.

03

Share and act

Send the debrief to your team via email, share a link with viewer or editor access, or ask follow-up questions about the meeting.
